# 👶 Baby Day — Labor Companion

A single-file, offline-friendly web app to time contractions, know **when to head to the
hospital**, and track baby's first days. Built for a first baby, no internet required once loaded.

## Get it on your phone (fastest)

**Option A — GitHub Pages (recommended, gives a real link):**
1. On GitHub: **Settings → Pages**.
2. Source: **Deploy from a branch**, branch `claude/labor-contraction-tracker-AoFtG` (or `main` after merge), folder `/ (root)`. Save.
3. Wait ~1 min, then open: `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/labor/`
4. On the phone, tap the browser menu → **Add to Home Screen**. It now opens like an app and works offline.

**Option B — open directly:** download the `labor/` folder and open `index.html` in any browser
(works offline, but "Add to Home Screen" / install needs Option A).

> Your data (contractions, checklists, baby log) is stored **only on that device** in the browser.
> Use the same device/browser throughout, and use **Copy** buttons to share a summary with your provider.

## What's inside

- **⏱️ Timer** — one big button: tap when a contraction starts, tap when it ends. Shows live
  duration, time since the last one, and rolling averages. Rate each as mild/moderate/strong.
- **📋 Log** — full history table + a bar chart of contraction lengths. **Copy** to text for your midwife/OB.
- **🚦 Go?** — the "should we go in?" calculator using the **5-1-1 rule** (5 min apart · ~1 min long ·
  for 1 hour), with 4-1-1 / 3-1-1 options. Clear status: Early → Getting close → **Time to go**.
  Plus a list of red-flag symptoms that mean *call now regardless of the timer*.
- **🎒 Prep** — quick-dial contacts (tap to call), a checkable hospital-bag list (mom / partner / baby),
  a guided **breathing pacer**, and a **kick counter**.
- **👶 Newborn** — after arrival, a full newborn hub:
  - **Now** dashboard: baby's age/day-of-life, next-feed countdown, today's totals, and day-by-day wet/dirty expectations.
  - **Feed**: one-tap breastfeed (L/R/both) or formula, with the **doctor's formula plan auto-calculated** per day (e.g. 20 ml day 1, +10/day, cap 120) and pre-filled.
  - **Diaper**: wet/dirty with poop colour + consistency.
  - **Growth**: daily breastfeeding-supply trend + weight tracking (% change from birth).
  - **Info**: baby name/birth time, an editable formula plan, and a "Is this normal?" newborn FAQ with red-flag warnings.
  - Everything one-tap, autosaved, editable/deletable, and **synced live** across phones (including baby name, birth time, and feeding plan).

## ⚠️ Important

This app is a **helper, not medical advice**. Always follow your own provider's and hospital's
instructions. **Call your provider or go in immediately** if your water breaks, you have bright-red
bleeding, reduced baby movement, severe headache/vision changes, or anything feels wrong — no matter
what the timer shows.
