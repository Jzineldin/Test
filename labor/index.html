When should we go in?

Most providers use the 5-1-1 rule for a first baby: contractions about 5 minutes apart, each lasting ~1 minute, holding that pattern for 1 hour. You said you're close to the hospital, so 5-1-1 is a sensible target — but always follow what your own provider told you.

Go in / call now — don't wait for the pattern

  • Water breaks (note time & colour — green/brown needs a call)
  • Bright-red vaginal bleeding (more than spotting)
  • Baby moving noticeably less than usual
  • Severe constant pain, or contractions you can't talk through that come every 2–3 min
  • Bad headache, vision changes, or sudden swelling
  • You feel the urge to push, or it's a fast/precipitous labor
  • Anything that frightens you — trust your gut

Educational guidance only. Your hospital's and provider's instructions always win.

How it compares to a typical newborn

General guides say per feed: day 1 ~5–15 ml · day 3 ~20–30 ml · end of week 1 ~45–60 ml · week 2 ~60 ml · ~1 month ~90–120 ml. Babies take roughly 150 ml/kg/day total over 8–12 feeds.

Your doctor's plan hits 120 ml/feed by ~day 11 — a bit faster than these averages, which can be exactly right for Malek's supplementation/weight gain. Worth a quick confirm with the doctor that it's truly +10 ml per måltid each day, cap 120. The doctor's number always beats any chart.
