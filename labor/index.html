When should we go in?

The 5-1-1 rule for a first baby: contractions about 5 min apart, each ~1 min long, holding for 1 hour. Always follow your own provider.

Go in / call now — don't wait for the pattern

  • Water breaks (note time & colour)
  • Bright-red bleeding (more than spotting)
  • Baby moving noticeably less
  • Contractions you can't talk through every 2–3 min
  • Bad headache, vision changes, sudden swelling
  • Urge to push · anything that frightens you

How it compares to a typical newborn

Guides say per feed: day 1 ~5–15 ml · day 3 ~20–30 ml · week 1 ~45–60 ml · ~1 month ~90–120 ml (~150 ml/kg/day over 8–12 feeds). Your plan hits 120 by ~day 11 — a bit faster than average, which can be right for supplementation. Confirm with your doctor; their number beats any chart.
